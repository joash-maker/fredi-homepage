01

Who We Are

Mediahubink is a West Yorkshire-based AI agent business that builds and deploys custom AI chat and voice agents for UK small and medium-sized businesses.

Contact: hello@mediahubink.com · mediahubink.com

02

What Data We Collect and Why

When you interact with an AI agent built and operated by Mediahubink — on our website or a client's website — the following data may be collected:

  • Name — provided by you to arrange a follow-up
  • Email address — provided by you to receive information or a callback
  • Phone number — provided by you to receive a call from the business
  • Conversation content — the text of your conversation, stored to give context to the business you are enquiring with
  • Technical data — timestamp, day of week, whether the enquiry was made outside business hours

We collect this data on behalf of the business you are enquiring with. That business is the data controller; Mediahubink is the data processor.

03

Legal Basis for Processing

  • Legitimate interests — to help connect you with the business you chose to enquire with
  • Contract performance — where you have requested information or a callback
04

How Your Data Is Stored and Shared

Your data is stored securely using trusted cloud infrastructure providers based in the US and EU, all of which operate under recognised data protection frameworks including SOC 2 and GDPR-compliant standards. Access is strictly limited to Mediahubink and, where applicable, the business you enquired with.

Responses in this chat are generated using AI. Your conversation is processed to produce a reply and is not stored by Mediahubink beyond the current session.

Mediahubink does not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party for marketing or commercial purposes.

05

How Long We Keep Your Data

Your data is retained for the duration of the client business's contract with Mediahubink and for up to 30 days after termination. You may request deletion at any time by emailing hello@mediahubink.com or contacting the business you enquired with.

06

Your Rights Under UK GDPR

Right to access

Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

Right to rectification

Ask us to correct inaccurate data.

Right to erasure

Ask us to delete your data.

Right to restriction

Ask us to limit how we use your data.

Right to object

Object to processing on the basis of legitimate interests.

Right to portability

Request your data in a portable format.

To exercise any right, contact us at hello@mediahubink.com. We will respond within 30 days.

07

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

ICO website: ico.org.uk  ·  Helpline: 0303 123 1113

08

Cookies

Our website (mediahubink.com) uses only essential technical cookies required for the site to function. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. No cookie consent banner is required.
